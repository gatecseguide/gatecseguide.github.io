Five papers, five different setters

A different institute every year

GATE EE 2022 was organised by IIT Kharagpur, 2023 by IIT Kanpur, 2024 by IISc Bengaluru, 2025 by IIT Roorkee and 2026 by IIT Guwahati. GATE 2027 is organised by IIT Madras, which set none of the five — so no counted paper shows how the 2027 setter writes one.

The 2027 syllabus changed one spelling

The 2026 and 2027 syllabus PDFs were compared phrase by phrase: nothing was added, removed or reworded. The one change is “Butterwoth” corrected to “Butterworth”, and the 2024 and 2025 syllabi name the same topics too.
