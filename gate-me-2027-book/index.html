What the 2027 syllabus changed

The 2026 and 2027 syllabus PDFs were compared phrase by phrase, by a program: 30 phrases added, 5 removed. The 2024, 2025 and 2026 syllabi are identical to each other, so the 2024, 2025 and 2026 papers were all set on the syllabus 2027 has now revised.

New in 2027

Additive Manufacturing and Computer Aided Manufacturing and Automation become paragraphs of their own, adding pneumatic, electro-pneumatic and hydraulic actuators and programmable logic controllers. Vibrations adds the PID controller and transfer functions; Heat Transfer adds boiling and condensation; the thermodynamics applications add the basics of combustion; production planning adds work study and Six Sigma.

Gone, or moved

Lagrange’s equation and Heisler’s charts are no longer named. The other three removed phrases reappear under new headings: additive manufacturing under its own paragraph, and inventory control’s deterministic models and safety stock under Production Planning and Control.

Past papers can only show the syllabus they were set on. For what 2027 names for the first time, the syllabus is the only guide there is.
