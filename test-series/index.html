Questions

Are these the same mock tests as in the GATE Guide books?

No. Each book has its own 10 PDF mock tests. The test series has 10 different tests, written new for the 2027 syllabus, so you can take both without meeting the same question twice. No question is taken from a past GATE paper either.

How do I open my tests after paying?

Your tests open as soon as the payment goes through. Your Razorpay payment ID (it starts with pay_) is your access key: it is on the Razorpay receipt sent to your email and phone. Enter it under “Already bought?” on this page, on any device, to open your tests again.

When are the tests released?

Mock Tests 01 to 04 are live now; new tests are added every week. Your access lasts until 31 March 2027, and you can attempt a test again whenever you like; your latest result is kept.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. On a phone the question palette opens from a button above the question, and the calculator and the numeric keypad fit the screen. A laptop or desktop is closer to the exam hall, so take some of the tests on one if you can.

What happens if the page reloads or my connection drops?

Your answers are saved on your device as you go. Open the test again in the same browser on the same device and it resumes where you were. The clock keeps running meanwhile, as in the real exam.
