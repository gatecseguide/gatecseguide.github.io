GATE ME Heat Transfer Formula Sheet 2027

By MD ANISH AHAMADUpdated 4 Oct 20267 min read

Heat transfer in GATE ME is steady and calculation-heavy. Conduction, convection, radiation and heat exchangers each drew between 9 and 12 marks across the six papers from 2022 to 2026, so none of them can be dropped. This sheet gives the formulas each topic runs on, a worked example for each, and the trap behind it.

Key takeaways

  • The four big topics carry similar marks, so learn all four rather than betting on one.
  • Most conduction questions reduce to one equation, solved for a plane wall with the right boundary conditions.
  • The lumped model needs Bi<0.1Bi < 0.1Bi<0.1 on the length V/AsV/A_sV/As​, not on the radius.
  • Many heat exchanger questions need only the definition of effectiveness and an energy balance.
  • Radiation enclosure questions start with view factors by summation and reciprocity.
  • Boiling and condensation are new in the 2027 syllabus, and Heisler's charts are no longer named.

Boiling and condensation (new in 2027)

The pool boiling curve plots heat flux against the excess temperature Ts−TsatT_s - T_{sat}Ts​−Tsat​. It runs through free convection, nucleate boiling (very high hhh, the useful regime), transition boiling and film boiling. Past the critical heat flux, a heat-flux-controlled surface jumps to film boiling, which is burnout. The Leidenfrost point sits at the minimum heat flux.

Dropwise condensation gives a much higher hhh than filmwise. For a laminar film on a vertical plate, Nusselt's result is:

h=0.943[ρl(ρl−ρv)g hfgkl3μlL(Tsat−Ts)]1/4h = 0.943\left[\frac{\rho_l(\rho_l - \rho_v)g\,h_{fg}k_l^3}{\mu_l L(T_{sat} - T_s)}\right]^{1/4}h=0.943[μl​L(Tsat​−Ts​)ρl​(ρl​−ρv​)ghfg​kl3​​]1/4

The heat released is m˙hfg\dot{m}h_{fg}m˙hfg​. A horizontal tube uses a smaller constant, with the diameter in place of LLL.

Using this sheet in the exam

Write the radiation network resistances and the fin formula on the rough sheet early; the exam-day rules guide explains what is provided. Logarithms, tanh⁡\tanhtanh and fourth powers all run on the GATE virtual calculator, so keep full precision until the last step. Many questions here are NAT with no negative marking, as the marking scheme, common to every GATE paper, explains.

Frequently asked questions

When can I use the lumped parameter model in GATE ME?

Only when the Biot number Bi=hLck\displaystyle Bi = \frac{hL_c}{k}Bi=khLc​​ is below 0.1, with the characteristic length Lc=V/AsL_c = V/A_sLc​=V/As​. That is r/3r/3r/3 for a sphere, r/2r/2r/2 for a long cylinder and the half-thickness for a plate. Using the radius itself is the standard error. Once the test passes, the temperature excess decays as e−t/τe^{-t/\tau}e−t/τ with τ=ρVchAs\displaystyle \tau = \frac{\rho Vc}{hA_s}τ=hAs​ρVc​.

What is the critical radius of insulation?

For a cylinder it is rc=k/hr_c = k/hrc​=k/h, and for a sphere 2k/h2k/h2k/h, with kkk the conductivity of the insulation and hhh the outside convection coefficient. If the bare radius is below rcr_crc​, adding insulation first increases the heat loss, because the extra outer area lowers the convection resistance faster than the insulation adds conduction resistance.

Is boiling and condensation in the GATE ME 2027 syllabus?

Yes. Boiling and condensation is newly named in the 2027 Heat Transfer paragraph, and Heisler's charts are no longer named. Learn the pool boiling curve with its regimes, the critical heat flux and burnout, the Leidenfrost point, and why dropwise condensation gives a much higher coefficient than filmwise. Nusselt's laminar film result for a vertical plate is the main formula.

Which is better for heat exchanger questions, LMTD or NTU?

Use LMTD when all four terminal temperatures are known or easy to find, for sizing an exchanger with Q=UA ΔTlmQ = UA\,\Delta T_{lm}Q=UAΔTlm​. Use the effectiveness-NTU method when outlet temperatures are unknown, for rating. Many questions need only ε=QCmin⁡(Th,in−Tc,in)\displaystyle \varepsilon = \frac{Q}{C_{\min}(T_{h,in} - T_{c,in})}ε=Cmin​(Th,in​−Tc,in​)Q​ and an energy balance, so start there.
