DSSSB TGT Computer Science Exam Date 2026: Shifts and Timings

By MD ANISH AHAMADUpdated 13 Oct 20266 min read

The DSSSB TGT Computer Science exam date for 2026 is fixed: the computer-based test for Post Code 41/26 runs from 27 November to 4 December 2026 in 18 shifts. This guide gives the full schedule, the shift timings, what the admit card will tell you, and how to use the weeks you have left.

Key takeaways

  • Post Code 41/26 (TGT Computer Science, Directorate of Education, Advertisement 03/2026) is examined from 27 November to 4 December 2026.
  • There are 18 shifts in total, at 9:00, 13:00 and 17:00, each two hours long.
  • 28 November has only the morning shift; 3 December has only the afternoon and evening shifts.
  • Your own date, shift and centre are printed only on the admit card, which was not out as of 13 October 2026.
  • With about six weeks left, finish the syllabus by mid-November and spend the last ten days on mocks and revision.

The words you will meet

  • Post Code 41/26 — DSSSB's code for TGT Computer Science in Advertisement 03/2026. Every notice, result and answer key uses this code, so search for it on the DSSSB website.
  • Shift — one two-hour sitting. Different candidates get different shifts; the papers differ, and DSSSB may normalise marks across shifts.
  • CBT — computer-based test. You answer on screen; there is no OMR sheet.
  • E-admit card — the hall ticket you download from the candidate portal. It is the only document that tells you your own date.

The official schedule

The schedule notice issued on 7 October 2026 lists these days for TGT Computer Science:

Date Day Shifts
27 November 2026 Friday I, II, III
28 November 2026 Saturday I only
30 November 2026 Monday I, II, III
1 December 2026 Tuesday I, II, III
2 December 2026 Wednesday I, II, III
3 December 2026 Thursday II, III
4 December 2026 Friday I, II, III

That is 3 + 1 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 3 = 18 shifts. 29 November, a Sunday, has no TGT CS shift.

Remember: the notice fixes the window, not your date. Plan to be fully ready by 26 November.

Shift timings

Shift Time Reporting
I 9:00 – 11:00 arrive well before the gate-closing time printed on the admit card
II 13:00 – 15:00 same
III 17:00 – 19:00 same

Every shift has the same paper pattern: 200 one-mark questions in 2 hours, with 0.25 marks cut for each wrong answer. The full pattern is in our exam pattern and qualifying marks guide.

Trap: do not assume the evening shift is easier or harder. DSSSB papers in the same exam vary a little from shift to shift, and normalisation exists for exactly that reason. Prepare for the paper, not for the shift.

The admit card

DSSSB releases e-admit cards on its candidate portal a few days before an exam. When it comes, check four things at once:

  1. Your name, photo and post code (41/26) — any mismatch must be raised with DSSSB immediately.
  2. Date and shift — copy them into your calendar the same day.
  3. Centre address — find it on a map and plan the route; Delhi traffic at 8 am and 4 pm is real.
  4. Reporting and gate-closing time — late entry is not allowed.

Carry the printed admit card and an original photo identity card. Read the instructions printed on the card itself; they override anything you read elsewhere.

How to use the weeks left

Six weeks is enough to qualify if you study by weight. Section B (Computer Science) decides selection, because the minimum qualifying marks apply to Section B and to the total, not to Section A. A good split of the remaining time:

Weeks before exam Focus
6 to 3 Section B heavy topics: networks, operating systems, DBMS, digital logic, web, teaching methodology
3 to 2 Remaining Section B topics plus daily Section A practice
Last 10 days One full mock every two days, revision sheets, weak areas only

Our 45-day preparation strategy turns this into a daily plan, and the syllabus guide lists every topic you need.

Remember: a mock under real timing (2 hours, 200 questions) is worth more in the last fortnight than a new chapter. Sit at least three before your date.

What can still change

Dates set by DSSSB can be revised by a later notice. Two habits protect you:

  • Check the DSSSB website's notices page every few days, and search for "41/26".
  • Keep your login details for the candidate portal saved, so the admit card download takes one minute on the day it appears.

If a notice changes the dates, the shift pattern usually stays the same, so your preparation plan does not need to change — only the final countdown does.

A sample countdown from today

If your exam turns out to be on 27 November, the first day, this is roughly how the time divides from mid-October:

Period Days What to finish
14 Oct – 31 Oct 18 Networks, OS, DBMS, digital logic, number systems, web
1 Nov – 12 Nov 12 Programming (C, C++, Java, Python), data structures, software engineering, teaching methodology
13 Nov – 20 Nov 8 Section A daily, remaining CS topics, first two mocks
21 Nov – 26 Nov 6 Three more mocks, error log, one-page revision sheets

If your date is later, the same plan simply gives you extra revision days at the end. Do not slow down early because you hope for a December shift — the admit card decides, and it arrives only days before.

On the exam day

Keep the morning simple. Read the admit card instructions the night before, carry exactly what it lists, and reach early enough that a queue at the gate does not eat into your calm. Inside, the first few minutes decide your pace: start with the section you score fastest in, and keep moving — 200 questions in 120 minutes leaves about 36 seconds per question, so no single question deserves three minutes.

One last practical point: save the official notice PDF on your phone so you can check it offline on the morning of the exam.

Quick revision

  1. TGT Computer Science is Post Code 41/26, Advertisement 03/2026.
  2. Exam window: 27 November to 4 December 2026, 18 shifts.
  3. Shift times: 9–11, 13–15, 17–19; 28 November has one shift, 3 December two.
  4. The admit card alone tells you your date, shift and centre.
  5. Be fully ready by 26 November; spend the last ten days on mocks.

Frequently asked questions

When is the DSSSB TGT Computer Science exam in 2026?

The DSSSB schedule notice dated 7 October 2026 lists the computer-based exam for Post Code 41/26 on 27 and 28 November and from 1 to 4 December 2026, with 30 November also included. There are 18 shifts in all. Your own date and shift come only on your admit card, so check the DSSSB website regularly.

What are the shift timings for DSSSB TGT Computer Science?

The notice lists three shifts on a full day - 9:00 to 11:00 in the morning, 1:00 to 3:00 in the afternoon and 5:00 to 7:00 in the evening. On 28 November only the first shift runs, and on 3 December only the second and third shifts run. Each shift is a full two-hour paper of 200 questions.

When will the DSSSB TGT CS admit card come out?

As of 13 October 2026 the admit card for Post Code 41/26 had not been released. DSSSB usually releases e-admit cards a few days before the exam on its candidate portal. Keep your application number and date of birth ready and check dsssb.delhi.gov.in and the candidate login from mid-November.

Can I choose my exam date or shift?

No. DSSSB allots the date, shift and centre, and they are printed on the admit card. Candidates cannot pick or swap them. Plan as if any of the dates could be yours - finish your syllabus and at least three full mocks before 27 November so that an early shift does not catch you half-ready.
