GATE ME 2027 by IIT Madras: What It Does and Does Not Change

By MD ANISH AHAMADUpdated 15 Nov 20267 min read

GATE 2027 is organised by IIT Madras, and none of the six ME papers from 2022 to 2026 was set under it. So there is no "IIT Madras style" of ME paper that past papers can show you. This guide explains why, what the six papers set by other institutes do show, and the one real decision IIT Madras has already published.

Key takeaways

  • The six counted ME papers were set under IIT Kharagpur (2022, both sets), IIT Kanpur (2023), IISc Bengaluru (2024), IIT Roorkee (2025) and IIT Guwahati (2026).
  • IIT Madras last organised GATE in 2019, three years before the earliest counted paper.
  • Any claim that IIT Madras "prefers" some kind of ME question is not drawn from evidence.
  • The paper's frame held under five organisers: 65 questions, 100 marks, aptitude 15, maths 13.
  • The 2027 syllabus is the one ME decision IIT Madras has published, and it is a real change.
  • Plan on the frame, not on a guessed setter's taste.

What an organising institute does

The organising institute runs GATE for a given year. Papers are set under its supervision by subject panels, groups of examiners for each paper. Papers are set by panels, not by buildings.

That distinction matters. An institute's "style" would have to survive a change of panel members and a gap of years to show up in a later paper. Nothing in the ME record can test whether that happens.

Paper Organising institute
2022 S1 and S2 IIT Kharagpur
2023 IIT Kanpur
2024 IISc Bengaluru

2027 IIT Madras

The wider facts about IIT Madras as organiser, including its earlier years in charge, are in the guide to GATE 2027 by IIT Madras.

Why one paper per institute cannot show a style

For every year but 2022, the organiser and the year are the same variable. IIT Kanpur set exactly one ME paper in the record, in exactly one year. So its 15 marks of Mechanics of Materials in 2023 could reflect its panel, that year's plan, or chance. There is no way to separate the three.

The same applies to IIT Roorkee's 2 marks of Engineering Mechanics in 2025, and IIT Guwahati's 10 marks of Industrial Engineering in 2026. Each is one observation.

Worked example: how far two papers can differ

A simple way to compare two papers is to take each technical subject's marks, find the difference, ignore the sign, and add the differences up. For 2022 S1 against 2023:

Subject 2022 S1 2023 Difference
Engineering Mechanics 8 6 2
Mechanics of Materials 6 15 9
Theory of Machines and Vibrations 8 2 6
Machine Design 3 2 1
Fluid Mechanics 8 10 2
Heat Transfer 8 7 1
Thermodynamics and Applications 8 7 1
Materials, Manufacturing and Metrology 15 21 6
Industrial Engineering 8 2 6

Engineering Mathematics was 13 in both, so it adds nothing. The sum is 2+9+6+1+2+1+1+6+6=342 + 9 + 6 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 6 + 6 = 342+9+6+1+2+1+1+6+6=34 marks out of 85.

Now the two 2022 sets, one institute in one year: they differ by only 4 marks. Papers from different years differ by 4 to 36.

Remember: One pair of sets from one institute differed by 4 marks; papers from different years differed by up to 36. The year-to-year swing is far larger than anything an "institute style" could be shown to explain.

What survived four changes of organiser

If one institute's paper cannot predict another's, five institutes' papers can at least show what did not change when the institute did.

Feature Held in all six papers?
65 questions, 100 marks Yes
30 one-mark and 35 two-mark questions Yes
General Aptitude: 10 questions, 15 marks, all MCQ Yes
Engineering Mathematics: 9 questions, 13 marks Yes
Materials, Manufacturing and Metrology the largest technical subject Yes
Every technical subject examined Yes
The split of the remaining technical marks No
The MCQ, MSQ and NAT mix No

Those first six rows are the safest things to plan on. The question-type rules themselves are common to every GATE paper, as the marking scheme guide, written for CSE but valid for ME, explains.

Trap: Reading the MSQ fall (8, 2, 1, 0 from 2023 to 2026) as an IIT Madras plan. It happened under four other institutes. It may or may not continue under a fifth.

The one decision IIT Madras has published

The 2027 ME syllabus is the only ME document IIT Madras has released so far. Its PDF metadata gives a creation date of 6 July 2026. Against the 2026 document it adds 30 phrases, removes 5 and rewords 19. It creates Additive Manufacturing and Computer Aided Manufacturing and Automation as paragraphs, and folds Computer Integrated Manufacturing and Inventory Control into their neighbours.

That is a real decision, and the first change of wording in the four ME syllabus documents from 2024 to 2027. It tells you exactly what is named: control systems under Vibrations, boiling and condensation, combustion, pumps, actuators and PLCs, work study, productivity, six sigma, and quality and reliability among it. It does not say how many marks those additions will carry, and nothing in the record can.

In one line: IIT Madras's syllabus supports reading every added line; nothing supports a forecast of their weight.

How the six papers reached the count

Every question paper and key behind these figures is an organiser's own file. The 2025 files come from the IIT Roorkee portal and the 2026 files from the IIT Guwahati portal. The 2022, 2023 and 2024 files were taken from the archive of earlier papers that the GATE 2026 portal hosts. Marks and question types were read from each official key, never from a paper's layout.

That matters for this guide's argument. The record is clean, complete and checkable, and it still cannot show an institute's style. The limit is not the quality of the data but the number of papers: one per institute.

What to do with this

  1. Ignore any claim about what IIT Madras will do in ME. None of the counted papers was set under it.
  2. Plan on the frame that held under five organisers, not on one year's division of marks.
  3. Read the 2027 syllabus line by line, because it is the organiser's own decision.
  4. Confirm dates on the GATE 2027 dates page and fees on the registration guide.

Frequently asked questions

Who is organising GATE 2027?

IIT Madras organises GATE 2027, and the official portal is gate2027.iitm.ac.in. IIT Madras previously organised GATE in 2003, 2011 and 2019. The organising institute is responsible for the examination as a whole, and papers are set under its supervision by subject panels.

Does IIT Madras set a different style of GATE ME paper?

No such style can be shown from the record. None of the six counted ME papers from 2022 to 2026 was set under IIT Madras; they came from IIT Kharagpur, IIT Kanpur, IISc Bengaluru, IIT Roorkee and IIT Guwahati. IIT Madras last organised GATE in 2019, before the earliest counted paper. Any claim of an IIT Madras preference is not drawn from evidence.

What has IIT Madras changed for GATE ME 2027?

The one public decision so far is the 2027 ME syllabus. Against 2026 it adds 30 phrases, removes 5 and rewords 19, creates the paragraphs Additive Manufacturing and Computer Aided Manufacturing and Automation, and folds two old paragraphs into neighbours. It does not say how many marks the additions will carry.

What stayed the same across GATE ME papers from different institutes?

Under five organisers, every counted paper had 65 questions and 100 marks, 30 one-mark and 35 two-mark questions, General Aptitude at exactly 15 marks, Engineering Mathematics at exactly 13, and Materials, Manufacturing and Metrology as the largest technical subject. The split of the other marks and the question-type mix moved.
