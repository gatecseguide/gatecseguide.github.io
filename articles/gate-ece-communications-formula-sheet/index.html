GATE ECE Communications Formula Sheet 2027: Formulas and Traps

By MD ANISH AHAMADUpdated 4 Oct 202610 min read

A GATE ECE Communications formula sheet needs about thirty formulas, not three hundred. Communications averaged 10.0 marks across the five EC papers counted for the ECE book, and half of its questions asked for a typed number rather than an option. This sheet gives you the formulas those questions use, each with a one-line example and the trap that costs the mark.

Key takeaways

  • Most lost marks in Communications come from a factor of two in a convention, not from a wrong method.
  • Angle modulation and MAP or ML detection appeared in all five counted papers, so their formulas come first.
  • Noise power through a filter uses ∣H(f)∣2\vert H(f)\vert^2∣H(f)∣2, never H(f)H(f)H(f), and white noise has two-sided PSD N0/2N_0/2N0​/2.
  • Antipodal signalling gives Q(2Eb/N0)Q(\sqrt{2E_b/N_0})Q(2Eb​/N0​​) and orthogonal signalling gives Q(Eb/N0)Q(\sqrt{E_b/N_0})Q(Eb​/N0​​); the factor of 2 is the whole difference.
  • Bit rate, symbol rate and bandwidth are linked by log⁡2M\log_2 Mlog2​M, and baseband needs half the minimum bandwidth of passband.
  • Entropy and capacity are in bits only when you use log⁡2\log_2log2​.

Random processes: what a filter does to noise power

Block error probability

With independent bit errors of probability ppp, a block fails when more errors arrive than the code corrects.

Puncoded=1−(1−p)n,Pfail=1−∑i=0t(ni)pi(1−p)n−iP_{\text{uncoded}} = 1 - (1 - p)^n, \qquad P_{\text{fail}} = 1 - \sum_{i=0}^{t}\binom{n}{i}p^i(1 - p)^{n-i}Puncoded​=1−(1−p)n,Pfail​=1−i=0∑t​(in​)pi(1−p)n−i

Example: an uncoded 8-bit block at p=0.01p = 0.01p=0.01 fails with probability 1−0.998≈0.0771 - 0.99^8 \approx 0.0771−0.998≈0.077. A (15,11) Hamming code at the same ppp fails with about 0.00960.00960.0096.

Frequently asked questions

Which Communications topics are asked most often in GATE ECE?

Across the five counted EC papers (2010, 2019, 2020, 2025 and 2026), angle modulation and MAP or ML detection with error probability each appear in all five. AM and DSB-SC and random processes through LTI filters each appear in four. Entropy, capacity and Hamming codes appear in three, all of them recent. Prepare those formulas first.

What is the bit error probability of BPSK in GATE ECE?

For coherent BPSK in additive white Gaussian noise, the bit error probability is Q(2Eb/N0)Q(\sqrt{2E_b/N_0})Q(2Eb​/N0​​), where EbE_bEb​ is the energy per bit and N0/2N_0/2N0​/2 is the two-sided noise PSD. Coherent orthogonal signalling such as BFSK gives Q(Eb/N0)Q(\sqrt{E_b/N_0})Q(Eb​/N0​​), so it needs twice the energy, or 3 dB more, for the same error rate.

How do you find the bandwidth of an FM signal for GATE?

Use Carson's rule. The bandwidth is 2(Δf+fm)2(\Delta f + f_m)2(Δf+fm​), where Δf\Delta fΔf is the peak frequency deviation and fmf_mfm​ is the highest message frequency. Written with the modulation index it is 2fm(β+1)2f_m(\beta + 1)2fm​(β+1). Check whether the deviation constant is given in hertz per volt or radians per second per volt before you compute Δf\Delta fΔf.

Does the power of an FM signal change with the modulation index?

No. An FM or PM signal has a constant envelope, so its average power is Ac2/2A_c^2/2Ac2​/2 whatever the message or the modulation index. Modulation only redistributes that power between the carrier and the sidebands. This single fact was the subject of 2010 Q21 and is one of the most asked facts in the section.

How many errors can a Hamming code correct?

A Hamming code has a minimum distance of 3, so it corrects one error or detects two, but not both at once. In general a code corrects ttt errors if dmin⁡≥2t+1d_{\min} \ge 2t + 1dmin​≥2t+1 and detects sss errors if dmin⁡≥s+1d_{\min} \ge s + 1dmin​≥s+1. A single-error-correcting decoder given two errors decodes to the wrong codeword.
