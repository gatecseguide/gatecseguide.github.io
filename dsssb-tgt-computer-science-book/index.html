Questions

Which exam is this book for?

DSSSB TGT Computer Science, Post Code 41/26 (Advertisement 03/2026), with the computer-based exam scheduled from 27 November to 4 December 2026. Confirm your date and shift on dsssb.delhi.gov.in.

Does it cover both sections?

Yes. Section A (General Awareness with Delhi GK, Reasoning, Arithmetic, Hindi and English) and Section B (all Computer Science topics plus the 10 teaching-methodology questions) each have their own chapters.

How were the topics chosen?

From DSSSB's own past papers: 11 TGT Computer Science papers (2017, 2021, 2023 and 2024) counted topic by topic, plus 12 more papers for the common Section A. The most-asked topics get the most pages.

Is the Hindi section in Hindi?

Yes. The rest of the book is in simple English.
